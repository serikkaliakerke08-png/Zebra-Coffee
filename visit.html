Location

Zebra Coffee is located in Astana at Mangilik El Avenue, 56. The coffee shop is situated near the EXPO area, making it a convenientspotfor visitors who spend time in this part of the city. The location can be suitable for meeting friends, having coffee, taking a short break or spending some time in a comfortable cafe environment. Visitors can choose from different coffee drinks, tea and specialty beverages from the menu. The address is easy to include when planning a visit to the area. Zebra Coffee can also be contacted by phone for additional information. The phone number of the cafe is +7 (707) 173-65-05. Before visiting, customers can check the current opening hours and menu information. The cafe offers both classic drinks and special options, so visitors can choose something according to their preferences. The EXPO location also makes the coffee shop an option for people who are already visiting nearby attractions and buildings.
