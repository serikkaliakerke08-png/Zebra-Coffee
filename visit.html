Location

Zebra Coffee is located in Astana at Mangilik El Avenue, 56. The coffee shop is situated near the EXPO area, making it a convenient spot for visitors who spend time in this part of the city. The location is suitable for meeting friends, having coffee, taking a short break or spending time in a comfortable cafe environment. Visitors can choose from different coffee drinks, tea and specialty beverages from the menu. The cafe can be contacted by phone for additional information. The phone number is +7 (707) 173-65-05. Before visiting, customers can check the current opening hours and menu information. The EXPO location also makes Zebra Coffee a convenient option for people visiting nearby attractions and buildings.
