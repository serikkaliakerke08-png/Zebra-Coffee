Our Menu

Zebra Coffee offers a wide selection of coffee, tea, and specialty drinks crafted for every preference. The menu features both classic espresso options and refreshing house specialties made with unique fruit flavors. Guests can try fruit-infused creations such as Latte Pomegranate and Latte Pineapple, as well as traditional hot beverages. For instance, authentic Kazakh Tea and Millet Tea are available for 890 ₸, while signature choices like Turan Coffee cost 1490 ₸. Our menu brings together familiar comfort drinks and distinctive local flavors, offering visitors various options near the EXPO area in Astana.
